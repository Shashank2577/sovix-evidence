DIRECTION 01

Instrument

A precision console that lives in the dock next to Grafana and Sentry. Dark-first for long sessions; the ground recedes and the numerals carry the light. Left rail, dense rows, fast restrained motion (120–180 ms), linked tracing across charts, a sort toggle. Says: this is an engineer's daily tool.

dark · dense · neutral-precise · layered panels · restrained motion

DIRECTION 02

Ledger

The audit document elevated to software. Serif voice, an “Exhibit A” with a seal, figures captioned Fig. 1 and Fig. 2, footnote apparatus in mono, calm paper-mannered motion (260 ms), a “quote this” affordance. Says: this is evidence you take to a board.

7.8% Inter + JetBrains Mono · dual theme · #5B5BD6

DIRECTION 03

Console

The Linear school done properly: layered neutral surfaces, one tuned indigo accent, tabs, ⌘K search, a working three-state theme toggle (system/light/dark), springy-but-brief card motion. Says: this is modern B2B software your whole org can live in.

dual-neutral · balanced density · neutral-precise · layered · springy motion
7.8% Space Grotesk + Space Mono · dark · #D8F34E

DIRECTION 04

Signal

Branded and opinionated — PostHog/Raycast energy. Engineered 1px borders, hard offset shadows, a loud chartreuse doing only interaction work, display-size numerals, diamond marks, expressive spring motion. The hue never grades the data. Says: this product has a voice and a point of view.

dark · bold display scale · branded-opinionated · flat+offset · expressive motion
7.8% IBM Plex Sans + Plex Mono · light · #1D5FBF

DIRECTION 05

Blueprint

A technical drawing of the engineering organisation: title block, measurement grid, indexed callouts ①–⑤, hatched exclusions like section cuts, figures numbered C-1 and C-2, precise mechanical motion with no bounce. Says: the product literally shows its working — so the interface is drawn like one.

light · dense-precise · neutral with drafting voice · flat · mechanical motion

The recommendation

Ship 03 Console as the product shell, and pull two organs from the others: the Ledger's serif-voiced finding treatment for the published artifact and board-pack surfaces, and the Blueprint's hatched sample bars as the signature mark everywhere. Console is the only direction that credibly sits beside Linear, Vercel and Sentry for a whole org — engineers get the dark theme, a CTO gets the light one, and the layered-neutral system will not date the way 04's chroma or a full drafting metaphor would. 01 is the strongest pure-engineer answer if the ICP narrows to platform teams.
