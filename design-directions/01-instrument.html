Conventions & caveats

fired: review_coverage < 50 · ruleset rccef86aa

Thresholds are configurable policy conventions, not universal performance standards.

Only GitHub review records count. Approval given in a plain comment, over chat, or in person is invisible, so a team with a different review habit will score unfairly low — verify against the listed examples first.

83 of 90 merged pull requests carry no review record. If this team approves changes verbally or in chat, that practice is invisible here — check two or three of the named examples before treating this as a control failure.
